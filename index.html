What We Do

At Little Voices, we believe every child deserves to be heard. Our speech-language therapy practice is dedicated to helping kids develop strong communication skills in a safe, encouraging space.

We work with children ages 2–18 on a wide range of needs — from early language delays and articulation challenges to fluency, social communication, and more.

Every therapy plan is personalized to your child's unique strengths, goals, and learning style.

Our Services

Specialized support for every stage of your child's journey

🗣️

Articulation Therapy

Helping children produce speech sounds clearly and correctly so they can be understood with confidence.

💬

Language Development

Building vocabulary, grammar, and understanding — for children with language delays or developmental differences.

🌊

Fluency & Stuttering

Evidence-based strategies to improve speech flow and build confidence in everyday conversations.

🤝

Social Communication

Developing the skills to connect, listen, and communicate effectively with peers and adults.

🧩

AAC Support

Augmentative and Alternative Communication tools and training for children who need extra support.

🏠

Parent Coaching

Empowering families with strategies and activities to continue progress at home between sessions.
